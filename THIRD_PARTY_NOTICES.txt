-------------------------------------
Voicemeeter Banana is a separate VB-Audio Software product, distributed under its own donationware terms. This application's installer and portable package do not contain Banana or its virtual audio drivers. If explicitly requested by the user, the optional setup guide downloads the unmodified official installer and verifies its publisher signature before running it.
